Runtime maps:
grass002_2k_albedo.jpg    - sRGB color/albedo
grass002_2k_height.jpg    - raw grayscale displacement/height
grass002_2k_normal_gl.jpg - raw OpenGL normal map for Godot
grass002_2k_roughness.jpg - raw grayscale roughness
grass002_2k_ao.jpg        - raw grayscale ambient occlusion
grass002_preview.png      - preview thumbnail only

Notes:
- NormalDX is intentionally omitted; Godot should use the OpenGL normal map.
- JPG maps were losslessly optimized and stripped with jpegtran.
- Current terrain shader uses only albedo and height. Normal, roughness, and AO
  are kept here for future lit/detail terrain material work.
