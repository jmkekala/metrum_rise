Street lights SL-MAIN, SL-MEDIAN, SL-PATH, SL-RES-STEEL, SL-RES-WOOD: original work.
Built procedurally in Blender (tapered shafts, bracket arms and heads from code primitives),
three LODs each, three plain materials, no textures. Modelled by eye after photographs of
Finnish street lights on Wikimedia Commons; no photo pixels, traced outlines or third-party
meshes are in the models, and the photographs are not part of this repository.
